## [0.19.7] — 2026-10-27

### Added
- **The director lens set grows from 24 to 45.** Twenty-one lenses were added, each written to the same depth as the original twenty-four rather than as a colour swatch: fourteen sections, a thirteen-key machine-readable style block, three prompt templates (Chinese brief / EN image-to-video / EN keyframe) and a worked example on the shared control scene, so all forty-five can be read side by side.
  - **Chinese 8 → 16**: Ang Lee, Edward Yang, Jia Zhangke, Jiang Wen, Johnnie To, Ning Hao, Stephen Chow, Wong Jing.
  - **Japan/Korea 2 → 7**: Bong Joon-ho, Kore-eda Hirokazu, Lee Chang-dong, Miyazaki Hayao, Shinkai Makoto.
  - **Western 14 → 22**: Ridley Scott, David Lynch, Alfonso Cuarón, Iñárritu, Wes Anderson, George Miller, Almodóvar, Guy Ritchie.
  - Group counts are enforced by the panel filter, so the three groups stay usable: 16 / 7 / 22.
- **Two of the new lenses close a gap that was already visible in the look layer**: `looks/ghibli-watercolor` and `looks/shinkai-light` existed with no director to drive them. Miyazaki and Shinkai now say explicitly that the texture follows those look cards and the lens only owns how it is shot.
- **The craft layer is now machine-checked for every new lens**, not just described in prose. The panel gate asserts, per lens: at least three contrast entries each naming the adjacent director and the mechanical difference; an ASL range in seconds, a transition bias and an anchor-shot plan in the cutting section; the four sound layers (music / ambience / dialogue / silence) plus which one carries the scene; and a worked example carrying both a weak and a strong prompt. The original twenty-four predate this bar and are exempted explicitly in the test rather than silently passing.
- **The genre layer grows from 21 to 36**, so the new directors have something to actually tell. A lens only owns *how it is shot*; the genre owns *what it is about* and *why anyone watches*. Three of the twenty-one new lenses had no genre that could carry them at all — Wong Jing's card game, Stephen Chow's mo-lei-tau, Guy Ritchie's multi-thread caper — and the rest were borrowing cards written for other things.
  - **New narrative genres (15)**: 赌局翻盘, 对峙困局, 多线劫案, 港式无厘头, 荒诞寓言, 县城漂移, 阶级困局, 青春距离, 废土追逐, 小镇异闻, 家庭情节剧, 古怪机构, 霓虹废都, 生存宿命, 社会横穿.
  - **Grouping attempt reverted; the genre layer is a flat wall.** A first cut at three universal categories (故事/情感/视效, multi-membership) was taken out again: too many of the thirty-six cards span eras and looks, so the assignment was arguable everywhere and an arguable filter is worse than none. The tiles render flat; discovery runs on the per-card `aliases` (`mo-lei-tau=港式无厘头（无厘头/港式喜剧/周星驰式/搞笑/扮嘢/反高潮）`) shown by `canvas_list_skills`, and on the chat side matching the user's own words to a card id.
  - Each carries the same ten sections the existing cards use (dramatic engine, pressure source, character strategy, audience payoff, scene granules, episode hooks, act structure, production difficulty, anti-drift, confidence) plus a **类型级负向词** block, and each names the genre it is commonly confused with and the mechanical difference.

### Added
- **The "Media Studio" preset mode now ships with this plugin.** Installing the canvas bundle mounts the mode in the picker (`order: 5`, director-first persona with runtime self-checks, guided workshop flow) plus the bundled **director-workshop** skill — previously a separate package `@ninkch/dsh-media-studio-preset`, now merged. The patch deliberately mounts neither the canvas nor ArtCraft plugin rows (double registration breaks the webserver); discovery of both still runs at runtime via `canvas_list_skills` / `artcraft_status`.
- **Conversation-triggered canvas now opens as a right-pane tab instead of hijacking the session.** `canvas_open` and node-run requests used to call `layout.selectPanel` (full-screen). All three trigger points now prefer `ctx.sidebarRight.openTab` — chat on the left, canvas on the right, no jump — falling back to full-screen on old runners; the left-rail full-screen entry is untouched.

### Changed
- **The look layer grows from 9 to 15 and is decoupled from directors — it is now a palette library, not a fandom annex.** The reference pack had its 200 directors organised into ten colour families, so the six families the look layer could not previously express were mined from those palette.json files: for each family every director's six primary colours (each with a ratio) were aggregated into a weighted cluster, and the top eight cluster HEX values became the card's colour block. Every HEX on the six new cards is that measured aggregate, not an invented palette — cold-noir's #1A232D is what twenty cold-thriller directors actually average.
  - **New looks (6)**: 冷调悬疑 cold-noir · 大地暖调 earth-warmth · 极简冷灰 minimal-grey · 巴洛克浓彩 aroque-rich · 高饱和史诗 saturated-epic · 浪漫柔光 soft-romance. All legacy looks got semantic groups (国漫/日系/港味/写实) alongside the new families (冷调/暖调/浓彩/柔光), shown flat in the panel.
  - **Decoupling verified end to end**: the same lens (ng_lee) composed with cold-noir versus earth-warmth produces prompt slices whose HEX sets do not intersect — the look layer genuinely constrains colour independently of who is holding the camera.
### Fixed
- **Genre cards had no grip on the finished picture.** Two separate holes, both closed:
  - **The genre's negative constraints never reached the video prompt.** `buildShotLockText` — the function that actually attaches skill text to image and video generation — only pulled one section out of a genre card, `常见场面颗粒`, because that is the only section tagged `prompt`. So `禁止漂移` (the "don't show this, don't let that stand in for story" list) was sliced as `story shot` and could never arrive, and no genre card carried a negative-word block at all, while `forms`, `lenses` and `looks` all did. The genre layer was therefore decorative on the generation side: it could shape a script, and had no say in what a clip looked like.
  - **The panel gate now requires both.** Every genre card must carry ≥4 negative phrases plus a `禁止漂移` and a `常见场面颗粒` section, and `buildShotLockText` pushes `本题材的画面禁项` and `本题材负向词` alongside the granules. Verified end to end: a `stephen_chow` + `mo-lei-tau` + `short-drama` pack now emits the mo-lei-tau granules *and* its seven negative phrases into the prompt slice.

### Fixed
- **Seventy-two dead `../xxx.md` references are gone from the lens pack.** Thirteen of the original twenty-four lenses ended every section with «见 `../cinematic-language.md`（要点）» — but `assets/canvas-director/` has no such sibling files, so a reader following the pointer found nothing. They were backtick text rather than markdown links, which is exactly why the gate never saw them. The references predated this release and were carried forward by copy-paste; they are fixed here rather than filed as a known issue.
  - **The annotation text is kept, only the path is dropped.** The parenthesised clause after each pointer was a distilled rule ("切点逻辑：剪辑点优先动作匹配与视线匹配；转场默认硬切；ASL 按情绪曲线分配，不许均匀"), not a restatement of the linking sentence — a check across all 72 references confirmed each target file's clause was globally unique. So the rule now lives inline where it was read, and nothing the reader needed is lost.
  - **The trailing «配套参考：» line became a bulleted «配套参考要点：» list** in the thirteen affected lenses (33 items), because a run-on single line of three to four stacked pointers reads as one sentence in Chinese and is unreadable once the filenames are removed.
- **The gate now catches this class of defect.** `test-skill-panel.mjs` gets a `!`-assertion for backtick `../` pointers alongside the existing markdown-link check — verified by planting one in `ang_lee.md` and watching it go red before reverting. The old assertion only matched `](../`, so a bare backtick reference was structurally invisible to it.

### Notes
- Ten of the new directors had no prior module in any local reference (Ang Lee, Edward Yang, Jia Zhangke, Jiang Wen, Johnnie To, Ning Hao, Stephen Chow, Wong Jing and Guy Ritchie); their lenses were written from scratch, the same way 徐克 / 路阳 / 袁和平 / 乌尔善 were originally. Every ASL band, key ratio, light-source convention and sound-layer assignment is stated as a number so two people filling a shot list arrive at compatible results.
- `test-changelog.mjs`'s hand-maintained `COVERAGE` list is cleared: 0.19.6 shipped (33eec44), so `main..dev` is empty and those four shas now live in the released section. The list has to be cleared at each release, otherwise the next time `Unreleased` gains content the coverage check stops falling back to the released section and reports shipped commits as missing.

## [0.19.6] — 2026-10-26

### Added
- **The panel now reads the skill configuration back from the project document.** `canvas_skill_pack({set:true})` from the chat side used to write the document while the panel kept showing its own `localStorage` copy — the "both sides share one source" claim only held in one direction. `mergeHostDoc` now compares `skillUpdatedAt` and writes the newer configuration back to the panel, mirroring how the series bible already syncs.
- **Asset-sheet nodes get their own slice.** Image nodes whose label ends in 设定图 (created by `assetSheetNodeSpec`) now take the `assets` slice instead of the generic prompt slice — imaging and material only, no storyboard or editing rules. Detection failure falls back to the prompt slice.
- **The fight-rhythm card is finally injected.** `moves` shipped two cards but only `camera-moves` was ever read; the storyboard path now carries `fight-rhythm` as well, on both the Host and client sides.

### Changed
- **「类型规格」is now「规格」** (English: "Delivery form" → "Spec") across the UI, Host tool descriptions, skill text and both user guides — 81 occurrences over 13 files. Code identifiers (`forms` / `form` / `skillForm`) are untouched, so stored configurations stay valid.
- **All three skill-profile default sets now agree the form is unset.** `pack.js`, `index-core-skill-profile.js` and the client previously disagreed (`'short-drama'` vs `''` vs `'short-drama'`), which meant the two sides had different ideas of "nothing configured".
- **Dead profile fields removed** — `aspect`, `targetSec` and `platform` had no UI controls and stopped meaning anything once the spec stopped deciding aspect.

### Fixed
- **Selecting only a spec injected nothing on the canvas side.** `buildSkillPackText` and `buildShotLockText` short-circuited on `lens`/`genres`/`look` but not `form`, so a project configured with just a spec got 0 bytes on every canvas path while `canvas_skill_pack` returned 4.7–9 KB — the two sides wrote against different rules. Verified: form-only now yields 4727 / 9087 / 7934 bytes for script / storyboard / prompt, and a fully empty profile still yields 0.
- **An unconfigured project was still injected with 5.5 KB of camera moves on the Host side** — the moves loop ran before the empty check, so `storyboard` and `prompt` always carried `camera-moves` even with all four layers empty. Moves are now only added once a layer card has resolved.
- **A kind with no matching slice returned a header-only shell** — `assets` for a spec with no assets sections produced 365 bytes of pure header. `buildPackText` now returns empty when no section survives slicing.
- **`canvas_skill_pack` accepted any `kind`** — `{kind:'nonsense'}` returned 「【画布技能包 · nonsense】」plus empty slices. The tool now rejects anything outside the four documented kinds.
- **A `readSkillPack` RPC shipped with no consumer.** The client reads card files over the static `plugin-asset` HTTP route and the chat side loads skills through the skills service, so the RPC (with its whole path-whitelist guard) was a second channel nobody called. Removed; its path-boundary tests now target the loader directly.

### Internal
- New regression assertions in `test-skill-pack.mjs`: spec-only configuration must inject on all three generation kinds, a fully empty profile must inject nothing (moves included), the three default sets must agree on an unset spec, the assets kind must return empty rather than a header-only shell, the storyboard path must carry the fight-rhythm card, and the tool source must validate `kind`.

## [0.19.5] — 2026-10-26

### Added
- **Nineteen Mixamo action clips converted from awplanet's FBX set** (idle ×2, walk ×5, run ×5, run-stop, turn ×2, jump, running jump, sit-laugh, dodge-back) and merged into the action library. Conversion is reproducible: `scripts/build-director-actions.mjs` runs FBX2glTF, resamples every track to 30 fps, and zeroes the root node's horizontal translation so an action plays its pose in place while paths and keyframes own the movement. The single-frame "pirate" FBX was dropped — it is a static pose, not an action.
- **A pose library of 41 entries, including 19 director poses replicated from awplanet's `DIRECTOR_POSE_RECIPES`** (relaxed / contrapposto / alert / lean / crossed arms / hands on hips / sitting ×2 / crouch / kneeling ×2 / prone / supine / point / reach / guard / surrender / hands-on-head / talking) — translated 1:1 from additive Euler recipes to the existing `BONE_POSES` quaternion pipeline, with ground poses carrying `rootY` so lying figures do not sink through the floor. A **Pose dropdown** next to the Action selector exposes the whole library on any selected character.
- **`scripts/build-director-actions.mjs`** converts awplanet's semantic Mixamo FBX clips into the action-library format. It detects FBX2glTF (env `FBX2GLTF`, PATH, or the `fbx2gltf` npm package's bundled binary) and skips with install instructions when absent, so the gate never depends on an external binary. The FBX sources stay in `_refs` (converted clip JSONs are what ships).
- **An engine-staleness banner**: `engine.js` is imported once per page load, so plugin updates were invisible until a full refresh with no hint anything changed. `ENGINE_VERSION` is now bumped with every engine change (`explorer-1`), and opening the director desk compares it against `sessionStorage` — a persistent banner with a reload button appears when the page is running a stale engine.

### Changed
- **Male body deformation is now visible at storyai magnitude.** The muscle multiplier per limb went 0.22 → 0.55 and `m_standard` moved to waist 1.24 / muscle 0.50 / hip 0.96 (a masculine taper); muscular/plump push further. A new `head` parameter scales the head/neck vertex region for child (+18%) and teen (+8%) proportions. Verified on Explorer: waist and thigh verts move ≥2 cm at `m_standard`, child head ≥10% of stature, feet stay planted.
- **Character colours are high-saturation** (storyai-style vivid blue/magenta/emerald/violet/amber/orange/crimson) instead of pastel — and the type colour is now the **render fallback** (`ob.color || mannequinColorOf(typeId) || clay`) across the WebGL paths, the Canvas2D fallback and the asset-rail thumbnails, so existing scenes pick it up without touching user data.

### Fixed
- **The directo desk UI and the male mannequin base, in one pass.** The male body types are back on the xbot base mesh — Explorer is a spacesuit character and grading it as the male asset was a mis-pick (it stays in the library, relabelled 宇航员). Node dropdowns (`MiniSelect`) now stop `pointerdown`, `mousedown` **and** `click`, so a select inside a node opens and works instead of the node swallowing the press and popping the generator dialog. Image nodes gained a zoom button that opens the original in the lightbox; video nodes replaced the redundant reference badge with a fullscreen button and the in-player control bar gained its own. The director-stage timeline dock shrank from `min(33vh,320px)` to `min(22vh,214px)` so it stops eating the viewport. A double comma before the closing bracket of `BONE_POSES` had created an array hole that crashed pose blending — removed.
- **Child and teen heads no longer detach.** The head-scaling deformation only grew vertices dominated by the head bone, leaving the neck at its old size, so the enlarged head floated free. The scale now applies to the Head/Neck bone skinning matrices before the skinning loop (Head at full ratio, Neck at half for a smooth transition), so bone and vertices grow together — head width scales 1.182× for a 1.18 child ratio with no tearing.
- **Newly added actions never appeared, and cached assets kept missing them.** `loadModel` returned a cached asset unchanged, and the asset had usually been registered before the action library finished loading, so clips merged silently never reached it. A cache hit now diffs the clip list and re-decodes the pack (already in memory) to merge the missing names before returning — verified: 17 missing clips on a stale cache, 21 actions present after the merge.
- **The media action keys sit beside the resolution chip.** The image zoom and video fullscreen buttons moved from the top-right corner into one bottom-right cluster with the ratio chip (button, then resolution text, in a row), hidden while the video control bar is up so they never cover the scrubber.
- **Object height is editable in the property panel.** The `dirHeight` label existed but was never rendered, so the only way to change a figure's size was editing `MANNEQUIN_TYPES` — and that table is read at placement time, which meant already-placed objects kept their old height forever. A height input now sits under Position for model objects and writes the object's own `h`. The 少年 / 儿童 tiers were also corrected downward (teen 1.60 → 1.35, child 1.18 → 1.00) because the head-scaling pass had been pushing their crowns above the configured height.
- **`ENGINE_VERSION` is now bumped per engine change** (`explorer-2`), so a page still holding an older module shows the reload banner instead of silently serving stale behaviour.
- **The gizmo origin is absolute and pose root motion stays on bones.** `applyPlayhead` had been adding a pose's `rootY` to `pos.y` on top of the bone-level Hips translation that already applies the same offset — a double application that moved the object's logical origin, so the axis rose to hip height whenever a pose with root motion was active. The offset now applies to bones only, the follow-body opt-in around `poseCenterY` is gone, and the axis sits at `(pos.x, pos.y, pos.z)` — a humanoid's sole centre — always, in both the draw and pick paths. The regression asserts the gizmo paths contain no `poseCenterY` and no follow switch at all.
- **Body-shape deformation silently did nothing on non-metre meshes.** The waist/chest/belly/bust bands used absolute metre coordinates tuned for xbot (rawHeight ≈ 1.8); soldier is a ~44-unit mesh and Explorer ~194 — the bands landed at ankle height and the deformation quietly missed. All bands are now relative to the mesh's posed height, so the same `m_standard` shape deforms every rig proportionally (verified: waist verts on the 44-unit soldier move with feet anchored).

### Internal
- Body-type regression data: colour and proportion keys for the 12 mannequin types, Explorer asset integrity, deformation magnitudes and the pose-library census are asserted in the new `test-body-variants.mjs` (registered in the strict suite). The Explorer pack (`models/explorer.json`, 1.28 MB) ships in `assets/director/models/` and is registered in the library index.

## [0.19.4] — 2026-10-25

### Fixed
- **The gizmo axis floated in mid-air.** Its origin had been lifted to the animated hip height so that a fall-down pose would not leave the axis behind on the ground. That was the wrong fix: the axis must sit at the object's **own coordinate origin** — for a humanoid, the centre of the soles — and stay there however the pose moves. The orientation was never the problem: it already comes from `objectAxes(sel)`, so the axis follows the object's rotation (tip the figure over and the z axis still points at the head rather than at the world zenith). The default origin is `(pos.x, pos.y, pos.z)` again, hip-following became an explicit opt-in (`sel.gizmoFollowBody`), and the pick branch takes the same origin rule so drawing and hit-testing stay aligned. A regression block now pins it: standing and fallen poses both keep the origin at `y = 0`, a 90° x-rotation moves the z axis off world +Z, and a 90° y-rotation moves the x axis onto world ±Z.

## [0.19.3] — 2026-10-25

### Added
- **A dedicated "技能" (Craft) page on the right sidebar**: the old one-line expert dropdown is now a four-layer visual console with **no dropdowns at all** — every option is an icon tile. **Director lens** (24, the top layer because it owns the camera language), **genre** (21 narrative + 10 sector cards, multi-select, swaps automatically for the commercial form), **delivery form** (8: structure blocks, ASL floor, rhythm, quality bar — **aspect is not part of it**), **look** (9: imaging, material, texture; empty = follow the lens), plus the expert as a tile row. Selection is per project.
- **All four layers feed every prompt-generating path, not just scripts.** Script nodes, storyboard nodes, bible drafts and asset-sheet prompts receive the capability-specific slice; image and video nodes additionally get a `[镜头语言锁]` block carrying the lens's style parameters and negative phrases. Unconfigured projects inject nothing, so existing behaviour is unchanged.
- **`canvas-director` skill**: 24 director lenses (each with 14 sections and a machine-readable style-parameter YAML block), 21 narrative genre cards, 10 sector cards, 8 delivery-form cards (with a parseable `spec` block), 9 look cards (palette hex, key-ratio ranges, negative words), a camera-move dictionary and a fight-rhythm card. Four Chinese-language directors (徐克 / 路阳 / 袁和平 / 乌尔善) were written from scratch because they were missing from the reference material.
- **`canvas_list_skills` and `canvas_skill_pack` tools**: the first lists the plugin's four skills, the full four-layer card inventory and the project's current selection — so any conversation that touches the canvas immediately knows what craft is available; the second returns the assembled slice for `script` / `storyboard` / `prompt` / `assets`, optionally overriding a layer or persisting the config (`set: true`).
- **`readSkillPack` RPC** for the client to load card files, with a strict path whitelist (`canvas-director/` prefix, no `..`, no absolute paths, `.md`/`.js`/`.json` only, must resolve inside the pack directory, 256 KB cap).

### Changed
- **The "专家" (expert) layer is gone.** It was a single `general` option whose persona restated — verbatim — the rules already in the `canvas-craft` skill (four scene elements, six visual elements, one extreme close-up per scene, causal hooks, collision points, the 180° axis, terminology), and it even said "when the skill disagrees, the skill wins". Injecting both meant saying the same thing twice and risking contradictory instructions whenever the two copies drifted. The hard rules now have exactly one source (`canvas-craft`); `buildExpertContext` returns `persona: ''` plus that skill's text, so every caller keeps working unchanged.
- **The image/video prompt block became a full four-layer enhancement, not just style parameters.** Previously the prompt path carried only the lens's YAML and some negative words, so the look's imaging/material/optics, the genre's concrete scene granules and the form's rhythm were all dropped — the first shot matched and everything after drifted. It now pulls `prompt`/`assets` slices from all four layers (lens style parameters + lighting rules, look imaging + material + optics, genre scene granules, form ASL + negatives, plus the three negative lists), ~15 KB instead of ~3 KB.
- **The delivery form no longer decides aspect ratio.** Aspect and resolution belong to the video node at generation time — 短剧 can be shot vertical *or* horizontal, and a short drama is not a licence to shoot cheaply. Every form card now states a **quality bar** (real key ratios, motivated light, readable depth) instead of an aspect; the `spec.aspect` field is gone and a regression asserts it stays gone.
- **The eight forms are re-cut along real delivery boundaries**: 短剧 (single 60–180 s unit, **multi-episode included** — a series is just episode after episode, so there is no separate "连续剧" form) · **电影质感** (a *texture* spec, not a feature-length one: ASL floor 1.5 s, 24 fps, every shot must survive a cinema screen; its duration tiers stop at 30–300 s because a video model only emits 3–8 s at a time — a 120-minute tier would be fiction) · **商业片** (宣传片 / 广告片 / 产品片 merged: near-identical needs, split internally by block ratios instead of three cards) · 纪实 · 音乐视频 · 预告 · 解说 · 访谈.
- **Every form card now states its generation-count arithmetic** (a video model emits 3–8 s per clip, so 90 s ≈ 12–30 clips; long takes are stitched with tail-frame continuation rather than assumed to come out in one generation).
- **The skill configuration is stored on the project document** (`skillProfile` + `skillUpdatedAt`), mirroring how the series bible syncs, so the conversation side reads the same selection the panel shows. Old projects have no such field and fall back to the defaults.
- **`canvas-studio` now opens with a skill inventory table** listing all four skills, what each owns and when to load it, plus the rule that every prompt should go through `canvas_skill_pack` first.

### Fixed
- **The skill panel showed nothing until the manifest arrived**: the card list was held only in component state, so the first paint after opening the tab was an empty page. It now seeds from the module-level cache and falls back to it on every render.
- **An invalid stored value could pin a layer to a dead id**: numbers, objects and arrays written into the profile were read back verbatim (`"[object Object]"`). Only string scalars are accepted now; anything else falls back to the default.
- **A project with no craft configured still paid a prompt prefix**: the config header was emitted even when no card had been read. When zero cards resolve, nothing at all is injected.

### Internal
- `assets/canvas-director/pack.js` is a **pure-function module shared by Host and Client** (section splitting by `<!-- slice:xxx -->` markers with a title-based fallback, `KIND_SLICES` mapping capability → section tags, YAML/spec/negative-word extraction, manifest validation, profile normalisation), so the panel and the conversation produce identical slices.
- New regression scripts: `test-skill-panel.mjs` (card structure across all five layers, reverse assertions that `script` never receives style parameters and `prompt` never receives narrative method, per-project isolation, three sidebar tabs, zero `<select>` on the craft page, 72-glyph closure, injection matrix across six paths) and `test-skill-pack.mjs` (`readSkillPack` boundary cases, four registered skills, both new tools end to end). The gate now runs 78 suites.
- Updated `test-panel-layout.mjs` (three tabs), `test-tools.mjs` and `test-skill-inject.mjs` (four skills, expert moved off the settings page) for the new structure.

## [0.19.2] — 2026-10-25

### Added
- **Action library for the 3D director stage**: `props.keys` keyframes so a prop (spear shaft, tip) can follow a hand through time; `autoRigMap()` maps any humanoid skeleton (Mixamo, VRM 0.x/1.0, Blender `.L/.R`, Daz/Genesis, Unreal, MakeHuman) onto 21 canonical semantic joints, so one action can drive any humanoid model without a per-rig allowlist; a collapsible **Custom action** panel groups IK, constraints, action layers and per-bone editing behind one toggle so the properties column stays readable.
- **Two-bone analytic IK** (`solveTwoBoneDirs`, `resolveIkAndConstraints`): cosine-law solve with pole vector, pivot-correct aiming that converges in 3 iterations even when the parent has non-uniform scale, plus `damped_track` / `copy_rotation` / `limit_rotation` constraints. Evaluation order follows FK → constraints → IK.
- **Mocap intake** (`parseBVH`, `mapBVHToBones`, `retargetBVH`, `sampleClips`): BVH hierarchy/motion parsing, rest-delta retargeting to pose keys, and NLA-lite layering with `replace` / `add` / `multiply`, `influence`, `loop` and `t0/t1`.
- **Dual-quaternion skinning** (`mat4ToDualQuat`, `dqTransformPoint`, `skinPointWithMats`): `skinModel` accepts `skinning: 'lbs' | 'dqs' | 'auto'`, so large joint rotations no longer collapse the mesh.
- **Keyframe interpolation parity**: Bezier F-Curve evaluation (binary-search `t` + cubic Bezier, handle-overlap correction), a full easing table (`smoothstep`/`linear`/`sine`/`quad`/`cubic`/`quart`/`quint`/`expo`/`circ`/`back`/`elastic`/`bounce` × `in`/`out`/`in_out`), quaternion slerp for pose keys and tracks, and per-key easing in `poseAnim`.

### Fixed
- **Poses and root offsets sat above or below the floor**: `rootY` (crouch / qinggong / knockdown) was applied in metres to a node translation measured in centimetres, so every pose that relied on it kept its feet off the ground (crouch was 30 cm in the air). It is now scaled to the node unit, which puts crouch at 1 cm and knockdown at 7 mm.
- **The gizmo stayed on the floor while the character lay down**: the axis origin was pinned to the object root. It now follows the animated hip height (`poseCenterY`), so the axis sits on the body in every pose while drawing and picking stay on the same origin.

Scene data lives in `localStorage` (`dsh-ic:director:<projectId>`, versioned, corrupt data falls back to the default stage) so the project document never carries vertex arrays; the exported still is the real product.
- **A storyboard is no longer one generation: it is cut at real shot boundaries** (`planShotSegments`): the whole table used to be flattened into a single prompt whose duration was the sum of every shot, so a 13-shot / 62-second table was squeezed into one 15-second request and the model could only compress — which is exactly why the motion came out as nothing but a slow pan. The planner now walks the shots and may only break a segment on a real cut — `scene_change` / `closeup` / `new_character` (or the table's first shot); one continuous `continuation` action beat always stays inside one segment because its motion phase cannot be split, and a segment that would exceed the per-segment cap is cut at the nearest legal cut point. When no legal cut exists it still refuses to cut mid-shot silently: the segment is marked `seamRisk: 'forced'` and the canvas surfaces the warning. Each segment becomes its own video node carrying `segShotFrom` / `segShotTo` / `segIndex` / `segCount`, so the prompt composer (`buildStoryboardVideoPrompt(sb, segment)`) writes only that segment's shots. The storyboard node shows the plan before you expand ("N segments / M shots / longest S seconds" plus a clean-vs-forced indicator) and the composer gained a per-segment cap (`segMaxSeconds`, 6–30s, default 15) — set 6–10s for weak-continuity models like MiniMax, 15s for Seedance 2.5.
- **Weak-continuity models stop being asked to match pictures** (`segmentNeedsHardFrame`): tail-frame chaining is now only used when the segment genuinely crosses a continuous action *and* the model can honour a hard first frame. Segments that start on a cut declare "do not continue the previous picture, establish this shot fresh" in the prompt, so the edit is a clean cut instead of a forced match — no double image, no stutter at the join. - **Shot prompts now carry the four things the model cannot invent**: `timeBeats` (three action beats per shot — wind-up → drive → settle, because one beat is performed as one static pose), `camera` (shot size + focal length + aperture + a move with a start and an end, instead of the empty "push in / pan" template), `vfx` (named physical effects — slanted rain, dust in a light shaft, coat hem in the wind — since an unwritten effect simply does not exist), and `negative` (face swap, drift, clipping, flicker, lip-sync mismatch, plus genre extras). - **The storyboard generator is told how to cut and how to layer motion**: the JSON contract now demands 3 `timeBeats` for every shot ≥2s, a single primary camera move written start→end, 1–3 named VFX, and a `negative` list, and it carries a new segmentation rule — never split one character's one continuous action into several `continuation` shots; when you genuinely change the camera, make the next shot a scene change / insert close-up / new character so the cut is clean. - **`canvas-craft` gained four methodology sections** (+169 lines, nothing removed): `timeBeats` (3 beats with a per-genre example table and a "a single beat = pan only" failure mode), `camera` (shot size × focal length, aperture × depth of field, twelve named moves with their trigger conditions, and a capacity limit of one move + one action chain per shot), `vfx` (five categories with writing examples and a "at least 3 shots per scene" floor), and `negative` (eleven generic failure modes with a positive-avoidance rewrite for each, plus genre additions). ## [0.18.0] — 2026-10-18

## [0.17.7] — 2026-10-12

### Fixed
- **Image APIs finally work from the browser.** All third-party gateway calls are proxied through the Harness host (`/api/fetch-proxy`) — browser-direct calls were blocked by CORS (`Failed to fetch`) because image gateways don't return CORS headers. Covers image `generations`, multi-reference `edits` (multipart), signed-URL downloads, generic video submit+poll, the **Seedance official protocol**, video downloads and TTS. Status codes, bodies and content-types are relayed verbatim; upstream timeouts surface as readable "timeout" errors.

### Added
- **The data root is now a portable data package.** Project folders use the **project name** (project ids stay as logical paths, mapped by the host at the I/O boundary). Each project folder mirrors `project.json` (nodes/edges/**series bible incl. assetIds bindings**) and `assets.json` (asset metadata index) plus media files, updated in real time. `canvas.meta.json` keeps the id→folder map.
- **One-click import**: copy the data folder to a new machine → set it as data root → *Import from data root* restores projects, nodes, bible and assets (newest-wins by `updatedAt`).
- Asset names follow **"node name + time"** everywhere (scripts, storyboards, images, videos, final cuts).
- **Asset deletion syncs the data root** (file deleted via the id→folder mapping + index entry removed).
- The asset index **self-heals**: the startup full sync replaces `assets.json` from the browser library (single source of truth; empty projects align too).
- Storyboard nodes got a manual **"Save as asset"** button; text assets (script/storyboard markdown) persist to the data root.

### Fixed
- **Project deletion resurrection**: deleting no longer pushes the doomed document to the host first (race), and the host now keeps a long-lived deletion tombstone — late `projectsPut`s (debounce races, startup snapshot pushes) are rejected. Importing a project clears its tombstone.
- Bible merging no longer drops `assetIds` bindings; `projectsPut` writes atomically and mirrors to the data root.

## [0.17.6] — 2026-10-12

### Changed
- **Storyboard workflow corrected: one storyboard = one multi-shot generation.** Expanding a storyboard wires it to a *single* video node; all shots (order + per-shot duration) are composed into one multi-shot prompt and the video model renders the whole clip in one call — no per-shot videos, no stitching seams.
- AI storyboard contract rewritten: 2–5 s per shot (one continuous action beat), durations sum to the target length (15 s ≈ 4–7 shots); default shot count 12 → 6; 12 s/15 s added to video durations; agent-created video nodes auto-connect to the latest storyboard.

### Fixed
- Node-deletion resurrection (three rounds): marquee/single-click/Delete-key deletions now record tombstones and push immediately; tombstones respect the in-memory canvas; undo/redo reconciles tombstones.
- Asset-delete confirmation dialog white screen; panel error boundary (crashes show a copyable card instead).

### Added
- Asset library: project scope filter with a **General project**, assets can move between projects, binding dialog v2; storyboards auto-register missing bible entries.
